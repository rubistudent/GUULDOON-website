About Us

Trusted & Integrated Services Across East Africa

Guuldoon Business Community (EA) Company Ltd is a Kenya-based integrated services company specializing in logistics, civil engineering, construction, and energy infrastructure across East Africa and frontier markets.

Our operational footprint is based in Nairobi, with extensive project experience extending into Somalia and the broader East African region. We combine local market intelligence with regional ambition to deliver end-to-end solutions.
